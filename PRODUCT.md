## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML/CSS served by GitHub Pages at the custom domain marcellostani.it. No frameworks, no build step, no JavaScript unless strictly necessary.

## Users

Prospective clients looking for senior technical leadership: companies and founders who need a fractional CTO or software architect without a full-time hire. They land on the site to judge whether Marcello can own the architecture of a SaaS/B2B platform and stay accountable for it.

## Product Purpose

Personal consulting site for Marcello Stani (Fractional CTO · Software Architect & Craftsman). Success is a qualified prospect getting in touch by email or LinkedIn.

## Positioning

A senior engineer who both designs the system and writes the critical code, then stays accountable after go-live. "Lazy by Design" philosophy: do things once, automate or design away repetition, fewer moving parts, lower maintenance cost. AI-native engineering driven by solid architecture and clear specs.

## Operating Context

- Bilingual: English is the default at `/`, Italian at `/it/`; hreflang and canonical tags must stay consistent between them.
- Contact is a `mailto:` to marcellostani@gmail.com plus LinkedIn (personal profile and the Lazy by Design company page).
- Based in Calabria, Italy; works with teams across Italy and remotely.

- Fonts are self-hosted Inter subsets (WOFF, OFL licence in `assets/fonts/OFL.txt`).
- Footer carries the VAT number (P.IVA IT09902710962) on every page, including 404.html.
- Copy is owner-approved: change it only to fix an actual error; otherwise flag suggestions.

## Evidence on Hand

Real, owner-supplied facts already in the site copy: 20+ years building products in production; technical lead at 4 startups; 1 platform acquired (EDGAR, acquired by Manet Mobile Solutions in 2022); 500 Startups accelerator alumnus (WeTipp); client/project work for Wonderful Italy, Tomasi Auto, RendersiConto (CTO · co-founder). No testimonials, case-study pages or client logos exist; do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility through specifics: real projects, real stacks, real outcomes, never generic claims.
2. Do it once: the site itself stays simple, static and low-maintenance.
3. Respect the visitor: no tracking, no cookies, fast and accessible.
4. Parity between languages: EN and IT carry the same content and structure.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
